Facilities

Everything you need to live, work and relax is in the same building.

Living

  • Fully furnished private rooms

    Bed, wardrobe, desk, chair and storage in every room – just bring your suitcase.

  • Communal kitchens and dining

    Fully equipped kitchens on every residential floor with shared dining tables.

  • Laundry rooms

    Washers and dryers you can book and pay for through the resident app.

Working and studying

  • Shared co-working spaces

    Hot desks, monitors to borrow and bookable meeting rooms on level 2.

  • High-speed Wi-Fi throughout

    Fast fibre Wi-Fi in every room and shared space, included in all packages.

  • Study pods

    Soundproof pods for video calls, online exams and quiet focus time.

Wellbeing and community

  • Rooftop garden

    A shaded green space with seating and city views, open from 6am to midnight.

  • Gym and yoga studio

    Open 24/7 with key-card access, plus free weekly yoga classes.

  • Social lounge

    Sofas, board games, a projector and space to meet other residents.

Safety and security

  • 24/7 security and key-card access

    Staffed reception, CCTV in shared areas and key cards for every entrance and floor.

  • Weekly community events

    Free events every week to help you meet people and settle into Singapore.

A typical week of events

Events are free for residents. Premium Stay residents also get a monthly pass for paid trips and workshops.

  • MondayMorning yoga in the studio
  • WednesdayCommunity dinner in the shared kitchen
  • ThursdayCareer and networking evening in the co-working space
  • FridaySunset social on the rooftop garden
  • SundayExplore Singapore walking tour

Accessibility in the building

The building has step-free access, lifts to every floor and accessible rooms on level 3. If you have specific requirements, contact us about a custom package and we will discuss how we can support you before you move in.
