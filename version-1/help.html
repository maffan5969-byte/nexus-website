Help and advice

Answers to common questions about our packages and moving in.

Frequently asked questions

Which package is right for me?

If you are staying less than a month, choose Short-Term Stay. For longer stays, choose Standard Stay if you are happy with a private bedroom in a shared apartment, or Premium Stay if you want your own studio and access to every facility. You can also use our package finder.

What is included in the price?

All packages include a furnished room, electricity, water and air-conditioning. Premium Stay and Standard Stay also include high-speed internet and cleaning. See the comparison table for full details.

Is there a minimum stay?

Short-Term Stay can be booked for 1 to 4 weeks. Premium Stay and Standard Stay start from one month. For longer stays, ask about a custom package.

Can my company book rooms for staff?

Yes. We arrange corporate bookings as custom packages with one monthly invoice. Contact us with the number of rooms and dates you need.

I have accessibility needs. Can you help?

Yes. We have step-free access, lifts and accessible rooms. Every resident has different needs, so contact us to discuss your requirements and we will arrange a custom package.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Premium Stay and Standard Stay need a deposit of one month’s rent, which is returned when you move out. Short-Term Stay is paid in full when you book.

Can I visit before booking?

Yes. You can book a viewing in person or a video tour through our contact form.

Can I have guests?

Residents can have day guests, who sign in at reception. Overnight guests can stay up to three nights a month in Premium Stay studios.

Moving to Singapore? Start here

  1. Decide how long you are staying. This decides whether Short-Term Stay or a monthly package is better value.
  2. Think about how you work. If you work from home every day, a package with co-working access will make a big difference.
  3. Book a viewing or video tour. See the rooms and shared spaces before you decide.
  4. Send an enquiry. Tell us your move-in date and we will confirm availability and price.
  5. Move in. Collect your key card at reception – your room is ready on day one.
