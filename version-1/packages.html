Packages and prices

All packages are fully furnished with utilities included. Prices are a guide and depend on the room and length of stay.

Premium Stay

For professionals who want their own space and every facility included.

  • Private studio apartment
  • Weekly cleaning service

  • High-speed internet included

Standard Stay

For students and professionals who want a private room at a lower cost.

S$1,650 per month

  • Private bedroom in a shared apartment
