About Nexus

More people than ever are moving to Singapore to work remotely, start a new job or study. Finding a flat, furnishing it and setting up bills takes time you would rather spend settling in.

Nexus takes care of all of that. Every room is ready to move into, utilities and high-speed internet are included, and the building has everything you need to live, work and meet people. Our team is on site to help, and key-card access and 24/7 security keep the building safe.
